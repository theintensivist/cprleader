How it works
  • Tap Rhythm check when pads are on. The check screen times your hands-off pause and gives the next step for the rhythm you pick.
  • The ring counts down each 2-minute cycle. At 15 s it asks for pre-charge and the next compressor.
  • Epinephrine: ASAP in PEA/asystole, after the 2nd shock in VF/pVT, then every 3–5 min. Antiarrhythmic after the 3rd shock.
  • A second epinephrine or antiarrhythmic tap within a minute asks you to tap again, so double taps are not logged as two doses.
  • Mis-tap? Undo sits under the keys for the whole code.
  • Big view enlarges the timer and keys for a tablet on the cart that the whole team can read.

      Vascular access

      IV is first-line in cardiac arrest. IO is a reasonable alternative when IV is not feasible or delayed.

      Advanced airway

      Confirm and monitor placement with waveform capnography. Once in place: 1 breath every 6 s (10/min) with continuous compressions.

      Log ETCO₂ (mmHg)

      Low or falling: reassess CPR quality. An abrupt sustained rise (often to ≥40) suggests ROSC.

      Voice commands

      The app listens for the team’s closed-loop call-outs. Say them as completed actions.

      CPR cycle

      “Rhythm check” · “Hands off”
      Starts the check and the pause timer
      “VF” · “Pulseless VT” · “PEA” · “Asystole”
      Records the rhythm. PEA and asystole restart CPR
      “Organised rhythm”
      Opens the pulse check
      “Shock delivered”
      Logs the shock and restarts CPR
      “No shock”
      Logs no shock and restarts CPR
      “Resume compressions”
      Ends the check for a non-shockable rhythm
      “Pulse present” · “No pulse”
      Answers the pulse check: ROSC or PEA

      Control

      “Status”
      Spoken summary: time, rhythm, shocks, drugs, what is due
      “Note …”
      Adds what follows as a note
      “Undo”
      Removes the last entry
      “Start code” · “Stop listening”
      • Orders, questions and plans are ignored: “give adrenaline”, “has adrenaline been given?”, “adrenaline in one minute”.
      • A repeat drug dose within 60 s is not logged by voice. Tap the key to log it.
      • ROSC said outside a pulse check opens the confirmation on screen.
      • The app ignores its own spoken prompts. Every voice entry is marked “by voice” in the log.
      • Browsers may send audio to their maker’s speech service. Do not say patient identifiers.
